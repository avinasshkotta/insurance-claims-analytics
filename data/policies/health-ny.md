## Coverage — Diagnostic and Inpatient Services

Diagnostic imaging including MRI is covered when medically necessary and ordered by an in-network provider. Inpatient hospital stays are covered subject to prior authorization for non-emergency admissions. Emergency admissions are covered retroactively with notification within 72 hours of admission.

## Prior Authorization

Prior authorization is required for advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET) and for all non-emergency inpatient admissions. Claims submitted without documented prior authorization for these services are subject to denial unless retroactive authorization is approved.

## Filing Deadline

New York claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date of service. Clean claims received within 90 days are paid within 30 days of receipt. Late submissions require a member statement explaining the delay.

## Provider Network

Services rendered by out-of-network providers are covered only in emergency situations or with prior written authorization. Claims listing a rendering provider not found in our network records are held for credential verification before payment.

## Deductibles and Cost Sharing

The annual deductible applies to diagnostic and inpatient services. Preventive services and emergency care are exempt from the deductible. Coinsurance of 20% applies after the deductible is met, up to the annual out-of-pocket maximum.

## Fraud and Misrepresentation

Any material misrepresentation or fraudulent statement in connection with a claim is grounds for denial under New York Insurance Law §403 and referral to the New York State Department of Financial Services. We will refer suspected fraud to our Special Investigation Unit (SIU) for review before any payment is issued.
