## Coverage — Collision and Comprehensive

Collision coverage pays for damage to your covered vehicle from a collision with another vehicle or object, regardless of fault. Comprehensive coverage pays for non-collision events including theft, vandalism, fire, hail, and flood. Both are subject to the deductible shown on your declarations page.

## Rental Reimbursement

If your vehicle is being repaired after a covered collision or comprehensive claim, rental reimbursement pays up to $50 per day, with a maximum of $1,500 per claim, for a replacement vehicle of comparable class. Rental reimbursement does not apply while the vehicle is held for investigation of a suspected fraudulent claim.

## Filing Deadline

California claims must be reported to us within 30 days of the incident date or as soon as reasonably practicable. Claims reported after 30 days require a written explanation of the delay and may be denied if prejudice to the insurer is demonstrated.

## Deductibles

The collision deductible applies per occurrence. Comprehensive claims involving theft carry a separate deductible of $500 unless otherwise stated on the declarations page. Deductibles are not waived for total loss settlements.

## Exclusions

This policy does not cover: mechanical breakdown, wear and tear, damage from racing or commercial use, or damage caused by a driver not listed on the policy. Loss of use beyond the rental reimbursement limit is excluded.

## Fraud and Misrepresentation

Any material misrepresentation or fraudulent statement in connection with a claim is grounds for denial of the claim and rescission of the policy under California Insurance Code §700. We will refer suspected fraud to our Special Investigation Unit (SIU) for review before any payment is issued.
