## Coverage — Dwelling and Personal Property

Dwelling coverage pays for damage to the structure of your home from covered perils including windstorm, hail, fire, and lightning. Personal property coverage pays for covered belongings inside the home. Coverage is on a replacement cost basis for the dwelling and actual cash value for personal property unless extended replacement cost is endorsed.

## Water Damage and Flooding

Storm-driven water intrusion through openings created by a covered wind event is covered. Rising floodwaters, sewer backup without endorsement, and damage attributable to foundation settling are excluded. A separate flood policy is recommended.

## Filing Deadline

Texas claims must be reported within 2 years and 1 day of the date you knew or reasonably should have known of the loss. We encourage reporting within 30 days so that documentation and evidence remain available.

## Deductibles

A windstorm and hail deductible applies per occurrence and may be percentage-based (typically 2% of the dwelling limit) as shown on the declarations page. All other perils carry a flat deductible per occurrence.

## Exclusions

This policy does not cover: flood unless separately insured, earthquake, intentional loss, wear and tear, pest damage, or damage from faulty construction or foundation settling. Landscaping and trees are limited to $500 per occurrence.

## Fraud and Misrepresentation

Any material misrepresentation or fraudulent statement in connection with a claim is grounds for denial of the claim and rescission of the policy under the Texas Insurance Code. We will refer suspected fraud to our Special Investigation Unit (SIU) for review before any payment is issued.
